    How the numbers work

    Independent student project. Not affiliated with, commissioned or endorsed by any of the brands or stores named. Based only on public Google reviews; figures are estimates.

    16,705 public Google Maps reviews of 14 Paris luxury beauty points of sale, January 2016 to November 2026. For department stores and Dior, only reviews about beauty are kept. 12,502 reviews have text; each was tagged for nine topics with keyword rules.

    The star effect of each topic comes from a linear regression of the rating on the topics mentioned, the store, the review language and whether a staff member is named. In the simulator, cutting a topic by X% removes X% of the reviews that mention it and adds back its average effect. Reviews without text are left unchanged, so a store with many star-only reviews moves less. It is an estimate of direction and size, not a forecast.

    "Visitors" are reviews written in English or another language that is not French, as a share of written reviews.
